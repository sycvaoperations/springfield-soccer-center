01

Introduction

Springfield Soccer Center ("SSC," "we," "us," or "our") operates the website at www.springfieldsoccercenter.com. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our site, how we use it, who we share it with, and what choices you have.

This policy is effective as of June 2026. We may update it from time to time — if we make material changes, we'll update the effective date at the top of this page.

Who this site is for

This website is designed for parents, guardians, and coaches of young athletes. It is not directed at children under 13. If you are a minor, please ask a parent or guardian to use this site on your behalf.

02

Information We Collect

We collect two types of information: information you give us directly by filling out a form, and information that is collected automatically when you browse the site.

Information You Provide

When you contact us or sign up for program notifications, you give us personal information directly. Here's exactly what each form collects:

Form Data Collected Purpose
Contact Form First name, last name, email address, phone number, player age group, topic of interest, free-text message Respond to your inquiry
Waitlist / Notify Me Email address, program name, request type Notify you when a program opens for registration

Both forms are processed by Web3Forms (a third-party form service) and the submitted data is delivered directly to our team inbox. We do not store your form data on our website's server.

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our site, certain information is collected automatically through Google Analytics 4 (GA4), loaded via Google Tag Manager (container ID: GTM-WQ68LRQ2). This helps us understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it.

  • Device & technical info: IP address, device type, operating system, browser type and version
  • Session data: Session ID, pages visited, time on page, scroll depth
  • Behavioral events: Button clicks, video plays, scroll events
  • Program finder interactions: Age group selected, player identity chosen, programs viewed, "Register Now" clicks
  • Cookies: _ga and _ga_* persistent cookies (2-year expiry) are set to distinguish unique visitors across sessions
IP address anonymization

Google Analytics automatically truncates the last octet of your IP address before storing it, which means the full IP address is never retained by Google's analytics systems.

03

Third-Party Services

We work with three third-party services that handle data in connection with this website. Each operates under its own privacy policy, and we encourage you to review those policies.

Google Analytics & GTM
  • Collects behavioral and technical data as described above
  • Data is stored on Google's servers in the United States
  • Used solely for site analytics — we have not enabled advertising features
  • Google Privacy Policy →
Web3Forms
  • Receives contact and waitlist form submissions on our behalf
  • Forwards completed submissions to our SSC team inbox
  • Data is not used for any third-party marketing purpose
  • Web3Forms Privacy Policy →
PlayMetrics — External Registration

When you click a "Register Now" link on our site, you are taken to PlayMetrics — an independent registration platform. At that point, you leave our website and enter PlayMetrics' environment. Any information you provide during registration (including payment details) is collected and governed by PlayMetrics' own privacy policy, not this one.

We have no control over PlayMetrics' data practices. We encourage you to review their privacy policy before completing registration.

We never sell your data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for their own marketing purposes. Period.

04

Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. Our site uses only analytics cookies — we do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

CookieSet byExpiryPurpose
_ga Google Analytics 2 years Distinguishes unique visitors across sessions
_ga_* Google Analytics 4 2 years Maintains session state for the GA4 property

These are analytics cookies only. We do not use them for advertising or to track you across other websites.

How to opt out: You can disable or delete these cookies at any time through your browser settings. You can also install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, which prevents GA4 from collecting data about your visits to any site. Opting out will not affect your ability to use this website.

05

Children's Privacy

This website is intended for parents and guardians of young athletes — not for children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under the age of 13. Our contact and registration forms are designed for use by parents, guardians, and coaches acting on behalf of players.

The contact form fields that reference player age groups (e.g., U3–U4, U5–U6) are intended to be completed by the parent or guardian describing their child's age — not by the child themselves. We rely on this reasonable assumption, but we do not implement a technical age gate.

If you believe that a child under 13 has submitted personal information through this website without parental consent, please contact us immediately at [email protected] and we will take prompt steps to delete that information from our records.

This site is governed by and compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) to the extent applicable. We recommend consulting an attorney if you have specific concerns about COPPA applicability.

06

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect only for the following purposes:

  • Respond to your inquiries: When you submit the contact form, we use your name, email, and message to get back to you with the information you requested.
  • Send program notifications: If you join a waitlist, we'll email you when that program opens for registration. You can unsubscribe from any notification email at any time.
  • Improve site experience: Aggregated analytics data helps us understand which programs are most popular, where users struggle, and how to make the site more useful for families.
  • Comply with legal obligations: We may retain or disclose information as required by applicable law or valid legal process.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond those listed above.

07

Data Retention

We keep your information only as long as necessary for the purpose it was collected.

Data TypeRetention PeriodDeletion Method
Contact form submissions Up to 12 months Periodic review and deletion from inbox
Waitlist / notify form submissions Until program opens + 3 months Periodic review and deletion from inbox
Google Analytics event data 14 months Automatic expiry via GA4 property settings
Google Analytics user data (_ga cookies) 14 months (auto) Controlled by Google; deletable via browser or GA opt-out

To request deletion of your personal information before these periods expire, see the Contact section below.

08

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have certain rights regarding your personal data. Regardless of your location, we honor the following requests:

  • Access: Request a copy of any personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your personal information from our records.
  • Opt-out of analytics: Use your browser settings or the Google Analytics Opt-out Add-on to stop GA4 from collecting your data.
  • Do Not Sell or Share: We do not sell or share personal data for cross-context behavioral advertising. There is nothing to opt out of. If this changes, we will update this policy and add an opt-out mechanism.
California residents (CalOPPA / CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to delete it, and the right to opt out of the "sale" of your personal information. We do not sell personal information. To exercise any right, contact us at the address below.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at [email protected] with your name and request. We will respond within 30 days.
